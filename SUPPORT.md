# Support

Earthler makes no promise of individual support. The project is in early development, with no version to install, so there is nothing to install or configure yet.

News is published at [earthler.org](https://earthler.org). The forum planned for forum.earthler.org is not online yet.

The issues in this repository exist to improve the project, as the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains, and they do not work as a help desk.
