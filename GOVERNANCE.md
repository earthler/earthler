# Governance

This file covers how the repository is run. The rules of each community that uses the system will be decided by that community, as the [manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) says.

## Who decides

The project currently has a single maintainer, Quim Pierotto, who decides what enters the repository and answers for the direction of the project. This reflects the current moment, when there is no code and no community of contributors yet.

## How decisions happen

Whenever possible, discussion happens in public, in issues and pull requests, and the decision is recorded there with its reason. Every change needs the approval of a person before it enters. Security and conduct matters are handled in private, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and in the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## New maintainers

Maintainers may invite new people after a consistent record of contributions. Maintainers will be listed in this file.

## What is not defined yet

The succession rules and the criteria for belonging to the movement and carrying the Earthler name are still open. The code, on the other hand, is protected by its license: anyone can continue the work from it under the terms of the [AGPL v3.0 or later](LICENSE), respecting what [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) says.
