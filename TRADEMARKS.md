# Name and logo

The code and the name follow different rules. As the [manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) says, using the code will be free, and belonging to the movement and carrying the Earthler name will require criteria, which are still being designed.

The [AGPL v3.0 or later](LICENSE) covers the contents of this repository, except for the `brand/` folder, and does not grant rights to the Earthler name or logo, as section 7(e) of the license allows.

## What is free

You may use, study, modify and distribute the code under the terms of the license. You may also use the Earthler name to refer to the project factually, for example to say that your work is based on it.

## What requires permission

A modified version may not present itself as the official Earthler, and a community or organization may not present itself as certified or as part of the movement unless the criteria exist and have been met.

The files in `brand/` were created by Quim Pierotto and are not under the code license. All rights are reserved, and they may only be used to identify this project. Anyone who publishes a modified version must remove the logo from it.
