# Earthler

Nov 8, 2026

Those Who Steward Their World. An Earthler is someone who takes care of their own world and takes responsibility for it, anywhere: in an ecovillage, on a rural property or in the middle of a city. The name stops saying where a person lives and starts saying how they have chosen to live.

## Where we are

Earthler is in early development. The first code is public on GitHub, and there is no version to install yet. There is no launch date and no promise of individual support. This text describes what we want to build. The project lives at earthler.org and on the forum at forum.earthler.org.

## What we believe

Technology serves life. Life belongs with nature. Technology exists to serve life, and life belongs with nature, so each thing needs to hold its place: humanity as purpose, technology as instrument and nature as foundation.

We believe technology came to take repetitive effort away from people and give them back time to live, to work for the collective and to respect what is unique in each person. A society can be highly technological and live in the middle of the woods, with gardens, livestock and buildings. We seek balance between nature, human beings and technology.

## The movement

The Earthler movement is the path to get there, in search of a more conscious way of living. It proposes autonomous and resilient communities, technology working behind the scenes, automation of what is repetitive, care for natural resources, cooperation, and knowledge that is shared and preserved.

You don't have to live off-grid to be an Earthler. You have to think beyond the grid. Nobody has to flee the world to be part of this. It is enough to take responsibility for the way we live in it.

## The platform

The Earthler Platform will be the first tool of this movement: a digital ecosystem for autonomous communities. We want to build an internet that a community of up to a thousand people downloads, installs on its own machine and reaches through the browser. Once configured, it should keep working even if the connection to the world ends.

In a single system, residents will be able to talk, make calls and receive emergency alerts. They will be able to browse Wikipedia, manuals, maps and content about their own region, publish lessons on what they know and watch public domain films. They will be able to search everything at once, mark on the local map what needs solving and decide together what is shared, voting directly or lending their vote to someone they trust.

The Mentor will be the voice of that place's knowledge, a local artificial intelligence, the same for everyone and most useful to those who don't know the subject. It will answer by citing the collection, help people decide and point out what the collection is missing, with an automatic check that each citation exists in the source and extra care on high-risk subjects. When there is internet access, the system will look for what was not found and propose what it learned to the wiki, which the community approves.

## What it will protect

What belongs to the community will be open to those who live in it. What is personal will be encrypted on the device: the administrator has no access through the screen, although whoever controls the machine could still, in theory, alter the code. Anyone who leaves takes what is personal with them, and what they published stays there, under their name. Children will only talk to authorized adults, under the watch of their guardians.

Each community will choose its own rules for living together, and the local law will still apply. Nothing will change in the system without approval, and everything can be undone. With internet access, each door to the outside will only open if the community approves.

## Truly open

The code will be open. Anyone who distributes a modified version will need to keep it open, and that is how improvements come back to everyone. We believe a social advance should be free and widely available, as long as every evolution is shared, as a way of returning the energy of prosperity and mutual care.

Using the code will be free. Belonging to the movement and carrying the Earthler name will require criteria, which we are still designing.
